I decided to go with a service called Plausible Analytics. What I found sort of off-putting about something like LogRocket was the level of information you can have on people. When I saw that it recorded up to two minutes for one of my sessions of my entire screen, I was genuinely shocked. I was shocked to see that such tiny lines of code gives someone that much access to what you do on a website. Taking this into account, I wanted to find a less "aggressive" analytics service, which is when I found Plausible Analytics. With their minimalist dashboard and simpler analytics, plausible is suited for more general use cases like basic marketing or blogs. They boast a very lightweight service that provides you with the only the most important analytics to save on carbon footprints, maximizing efficiency. With a very clean and easy-to-understand UI, it stood out to me as the kind of analytics page which gives me the right amount of information to understand the people who come to my page without putting an entire spyglass up to their monitors or needlessly blowing CO2 into our atmosphere.
